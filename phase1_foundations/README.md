# Phase 1 - Foundations

Discrete Mathematics + Probability.  Six to eight weeks in the roadmap.

Discrete math teaches precise thinking, logic, sets, combinatorics,
graphs, proofs, and probability sits right on top of it.  Without this
phase everything after is arithmetic on intuition.

| Project | Folder | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 - Logic & Combinatorics Toolkit | `project1_logic_combinatorics/` | done |
| 2 - Probability Simulator & Bayes Engine | `project2_probability/` | done |

Theory sources from the roadmap: MIT 6.042J and Rosen's *Discrete
Mathematics and Its Applications*, plus Blitzstein's Stat 110 for the
probability side.
